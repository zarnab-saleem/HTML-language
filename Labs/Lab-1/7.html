Computer Information

        Importance of Computer :
        Fast and Efficient Work:
        Computers help in completing tasks quickly and accurately. They save time and reduce human effort.

        Communication:
        Computers are essential for communication through emails, social media, video calls, and messaging.

        Education:
        Computers are used in schools and colleges for learning, online classes, assignments, and research.

        Storage of Information:
        Computers store large amounts of data safely, including documents, photos, videos, and software.

        Business and Banking:
        All major business and banking tasks are done through computers — from transactions to data management.
